# Module 1: Foundations of AI Collaboration

> Building the mindset and skills to effectively collaborate with AI assistants

## Overview

This module establishes the fundamental concepts and practices needed to effectively collaborate with AI in software development. We'll explore the mental models for working with AI, introducing the paradigm shift from viewing AI as a simple tool to treating it as a team member.

Participants will gain hands-on experience with various AI coding tools, learn basic prompting patterns, and understand the capabilities and limitations of some current AI systems. We'll also address important considerations around legal, ethical, and intellectual property aspects of AI-assisted development, and establish principles for balancing control and delegation when working with AI.

### 1. Mental Models for AI Collaboration: Roles/Personas

AI assistants act differently depending on the **role** you give them.

| Role | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Coder | Writes code from context |
| Planner | Structures ideas |
| Architect | Designs systems |
| UI Designer | Handles layout/UX |
| Reviewer | Finds flaws |

---

#### 🛠️ Role + Action + Context = Good Output

Be clear about **who** you want the AI to be, and **what** you want the AI to do.

> ✳️ Example:  
> "Act as a senior Python dev, refactor the EventHandler class for readability and performance."
